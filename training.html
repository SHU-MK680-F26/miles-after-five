First 5K: an 8-week beginner plan

New to running? Start here. This run-walk plan builds up week by week to running a 5K. It works for every pace, with three short runs a week and plenty of rest days.

Training goalFinish your first 5K (3.1 miles) at your own pace, running or run-walking.
Length8 weeks, 3 runs a week, about 25 to 40 minutes each.
Who it's forFirst-timers and anyone coming back after a break. Walking breaks always count.

Your weekly schedule

Every week follows the same pattern: three run days with a rest day after each, and an easy walk if you feel like moving. Swap days around to fit your work week; just keep a rest day between runs.

Recommended run days: Monday, Wednesday, Saturday. Recommended rest days: Tuesday, Thursday, Friday (or an easy walk), Sunday.

Week by week

Every run day starts with a 5-minute brisk walk and ends with a 5-minute easy walk. Do each week's session on all three run days. Run at a pace you can chat at. If a week feels like a lot, repeat it. There's no rush.

  1. Week 1

    Get moving

    Run 1 minute, walk 2 minutes. Repeat 8 times.

  2. Week 2

    Settle in

    Run 2 minutes, walk 2 minutes. Repeat 6 times.

  3. Week 3

    Longer run segments

    Run 3 minutes, walk 2 minutes. Repeat 5 times.

  4. Week 4

    Building up

    Run 5 minutes, walk 2 minutes. Repeat 4 times.

  5. Week 5

    Halfway there

    Run 8 minutes, walk 2 minutes. Repeat 3 times.

  6. Week 6

    Fewer walk breaks

    Run 10 minutes, walk 2 minutes. Repeat 2 times, then run 5 more minutes.

  7. Week 7

    Long stretches

    Run 15 minutes, walk 2 minutes, run 10 minutes.

  8. Week 8

    Race week

    Monday and Wednesday: run 20 minutes, easy and chatty. Saturday: race day, your first 5K. Run, walk, or both. We'll meet you at mile one.

Pick your race-day 5K about 8 weeks out, then grab a friend and start Week 1 together.
